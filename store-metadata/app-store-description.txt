Xota is a collection of simple, fast games designed for quick play sessions in Tigrinya.

Play arcade challenges, reaction games, classic board-style games, and score-based games from one clean home screen. Xota includes games such as jumping, dodging, star catching, target tapping, flying, tic-tac-toe, connect-four style play, and snake.

Features:
- Multiple playable games in one app
- Tigrinya game labels and in-game text
- Light, dark, and system appearance options
- Score tracking and replay-friendly sessions
- Simple touch controls designed for iPhone and iPad

Xota is built for casual play: open the app, choose a game, and start playing right away.
